# Floral Curtain Validation

After clicking Open Invitation and waiting for the intro to finish, the opened scroll journey begins with a dense vertical floral corridor: full blue, white, lavender, and soft-pink clusters fill the upper and side edges, while a pale central opening remains available for the invitation reveal. The corridor visibly separates into stacked left/right floral banks and a lower base. The first viewport is no longer a sparse edge-only treatment; the flowers read as a closed-to-open curtain composition and the central reveal becomes clearer deeper in the journey.
